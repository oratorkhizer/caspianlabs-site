Refund Policy

Caspian Diagnostic Centre — a unit of Caspian Lifesciences Private Limited

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This Refund Policy applies to payments made to Caspian Lifesciences Private Limited (Caspian Diagnostic Centre) for diagnostic tests, packages and services.

Eligibility for refund

  • Before sample collection / test processing: If you cancel a booking before the sample is collected or the test is processed, you are eligible for a full refund.
  • After sample collection / processing: Once a sample has been collected and/or the test has been processed, the cost of that test is non-refundable, as laboratory resources and reagents have been consumed.
  • Duplicate or failed payments: If you are charged more than once for the same booking, or money is deducted but the booking is not confirmed, the excess/failed amount is fully refunded.
  • Service not rendered: If we are unable to provide a booked test, a full refund is issued for that test.

How refunds are processed

  • Approved refunds are made to the original payment method.
  • Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days of approval; the time for the amount to reflect depends on your bank/payment provider.

How to request a refund

Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 90593 41154, call the same number, or email info@caspianlabs.in with your booking details. Please see also our Cancellation Policy.
