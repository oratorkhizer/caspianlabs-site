Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what patients ask us most. Need something else? Message us on WhatsApp — we're open 24/7.

Do I need to fast before my blood test?

Some tests such as fasting blood sugar and lipid profile need 8–12 hours of fasting, while many others don't. Message us on WhatsApp with your test name and we'll confirm whether fasting is required.

Do you offer home sample collection?

Yes — our trained phlebotomists collect samples from your home, free for orders above ₹500. Book online in the "All Tests" section and choose Home collection (or message us on WhatsApp with your area and preferred time), and our team will confirm your slot.

When will I receive my report?

Most routine reports are ready the same day or within 24 hours, and are delivered directly on WhatsApp. Timing can vary for specialised tests.

What are your timings?

We're open 24/7, every day of the week.
